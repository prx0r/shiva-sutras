## The pick (`excavations/ESSAY0.md` — 7-step run)
Master question first (clear, the corpus's own word), then the sharpened
pick: cutting audit (thread present across sessions?), completion test
(does the translation read as the answer, cold?), counter test (voice
the strongest cold-listener objection and meet it in the cell or flag
it for B-depth). Keep alternates (they're cheap, re-picks are not).

## The stack (`build/ESSAY0_OUTPUT.md` — corrected format, M3)
Per cell, in fixed order: **Q** (2 breaths) → **SANSKRIT** (text's own
answer, held form, line-ref) → **TRANSLATION** (existing exact rendering:
lecturer > critical > gloss, never composed) → **PLAIN** (mechanical key
output) → **ARCHIVE VOICE** (Grade A, ≤25s, completes the question).
Voice may read OR explain (precedent: either carries, rows 1–2 never
substitute). One cell per section + HEAD. Cell clips are grade A by law
(assert every cell-uid == A whenever cells are built/edited — M22).

## The plain state

sign-off). Regenerate + rehash at key sign-off (`plain_filter.py`),
never hand-edit. Sanskrit restoration (diacritics) happens ONLY at the
print-verify step against a print source, then card rows freeze.
