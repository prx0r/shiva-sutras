# 10 — FORMAL CHANT (Track F). Verse order, four blocks, no selectivity.

Track F is the fidelity object: every sūtra in sūtra order, same four
blocks each. It is built, not argued — structure comes from the text,
voices seat by the speaker law, gaps stay flagged. Artifact:
`build/TRACK_F_STORYBOARD.md` (one row per verse; separate from every
Track S artifact by law).

## Tagging (how the 77 rows were derived — repeat per corpus)
1. **Sūtra list**: Sanskrit (transliterated) + translation per verse from
   the most complete public listing (here: Lakshmanjoo rendering via the
   Kumuda page, 76/77 rows — page skips S1-19, flagged). Sanskrit forms
   cross-checked against the chant audio, never against ASR text.
2. **Readout text**: Dyczkowski front-listing per Light (22/10/45 keys;
   page-wrap artifacts patched: S2-4 rejoined, trailing spillover
   trimmed, blank numbers left as FLAG fill-from-commentary — never
   invented).
3. **Chant windows**: section runs located in the chant recording (here:
   Sharma S1 267–382 s, S2 392–442 s, S3 453–684 s), per-verse splits
   proportional inside runs. PROVISIONAL by construction — snap every
   boundary to the nearest pause at cut, then replace proportional with
   measured in the manifest.
4. **B3 slots**: anchors only where the corpus already proves them (here:
   the 6 essay-0 cells mapped to their sūtras). All other verses carry
   their section pool + "assign at cut" — assignment happens with audio
   present, reading the join, never from text order alone.
5. **B4 slots**: third voices mapped where they strike the verse directly
   (here: Osho discourses D1/D3/D4/D5/D6, Hareesh ep78 at the S1
   doorway, Sharma overview at section heads). Relevance sentence
   mandatory per insertion ("this widens the same verse"); failures
   stay out. Rights per `audio/SOURCES.md` — cut-only sources never
   redistribute.

## Spacing (flow consideration, not taste)
Verses flow without bells between them: chant (~6 s) → 1.2 s → T+P
(~10 s TTS) → 1.2 s → B3 clip(s) → B4? → 2.5 s water → next verse.
Bells, questions, and cards live at SECTION heads (from BEATS), closers
land each section's payoff (row them at assembly with lever flags).
Estimated full-77 runtime ≈ 2 h at full B3+B4 weight — the essence cut
(6 seeds) ships first; the full walk is a separate slow object. Never
pad thin verses; never cut audited membership for runtime (owner
decides band vs membership at ear).

## What this stage does NOT do
No reordering, no selectivity, no argument, no Track S references.
If a verse lacks explanation audio, its B3 stays a flagged gap — the
row ships short before it ships invented.
