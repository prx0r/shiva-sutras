# ROLES — the voices any build may cast (abstract) + our casting (concrete).

A role is a job, not a person. Same role = same voice within a build.
No voice speaks outside its role — that is how slop enters. TTS may
stand in for any role at v1 (marked placeholder); archive/human voices
own their roles at ship.

## The abstract company (portable to any corpus)
| role | job | owns | never |
|---|---|---|---|
| CHANTER | voices source-language text, recited/sung | B1; ALL sung source language | explains, translates, narrates |
| READER | voices source text, spoken (verse or commentary) | verbatim readings | paraphrase, doctrine of its own |
| TRANSLATOR | speaks the translation + plain lines | B2 card stack (T+P); English-first always | source language, invention, connective tissue |
| EXPLAINER | archive voice expanding the verse | B3; commentary already in the rendition, selected never written | cards, questions |
| WITNESS | third-voice testimony, non-direct | B4 only, with a written relevance sentence | card authority, doctrine |
| QUESTIONER | asks the spine questions, opens sections | Q lines; bridges marked HUMAN | answers, verses |
| HUMAN | owner: final voice + bridge writer | questions/cards at ship; rare marked bridges | verses (archive owns them) |

Handover law: QUESTIONER → CHANTER → TRANSLATOR → EXPLAINER flows by
design (question, tongue-switch, comprehension landing, expansion).
TRANSLATOR → EXPLAINER is the sensitive joint (timbre/room change):
gap + fades now, room-tone beds at ship. WITNESS always arrives framed
(relevance stated or bridge-marked), never cold.
